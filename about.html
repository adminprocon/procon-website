About Procon Infra LLP

Engineering-led EPC delivery for industrial and infrastructure projects

We focus on composite construction, industrial civil works, PEB and structural steel, and MEP, with project execution driven by planning, constructability, safety, quality and commercial control. Single-point coordination carries every project from design development through procurement, site execution, testing and close-out.

How we work

One loop, four disciplines, zero handoff loss

Engineering

Design coordination, structural solutions, detailing and analysis, constructability review.

Procurement

Vendor coordination, material planning, expediting, logistics and supply chain management.

Construction

Civil, steel and composite works, PEB and structural steel erection, MEP and infrastructure execution.

Handover

Testing and commissioning, snag closure, documentation, final completion and client handover.
